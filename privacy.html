Nu Alpha Phi · 985

Privacy policy

Effective November 3, 2026

This policy covers the 985 app and its connected Nu Alpha Phi brotherhood portal features. Nu Alpha Phi operates these services to help members connect, communicate, and organize fraternity activities.

Information we collect

  • Account and membership information: your name, email, phone number, pledge name and number, chapter, class, crossing information, membership application, approval status, and assigned roles. Firebase Authentication manages your sign-in credentials.
  • Profile information you provide: profile photo, biography, birthday, school, education, employment, family relationships, and social-media profile links.
  • Content and activity: posts, comments, messages, photos, reactions, polls and votes, event RSVPs, plus-one details, ticket/check-in records, submitted forms, and documents you upload. Please obtain a plus-one’s permission before entering their name or phone number.
  • Preferences and technical information: notification choices, push-notification tokens, read markers, blocked accounts, muted channels, saved items, and operational information needed to deliver and secure the service.
  • Optional location: with permission, the app requests location when opened and shares an approximate area with brothers. The map stores rounded location cells and an area label rather than an exact street address. Approximate location can still reveal your neighborhood. Shared areas stop appearing after seven days without an update. You can turn sharing off in Settings.

How we use information

We use this information to verify membership, provide profiles and communication, run events and check-ins, deliver notifications, maintain the directory and roster, manage access, investigate reports, and operate and secure the service. Shared birthdays may be used for birthday posts that identify your age and chapter.

Who can see it

Approved brothers can see member profiles, the shared feed, directory information, and locations you choose to share. Private channels are limited by chapter, crossing group, or assigned role. Direct and group messages are available to their participants. Administrators review applications and content reports; when you report a private message, its content is shared with administrators for investigation.

Published event details can be viewed publicly on the website. Comment access requires sign-in, and attendee lists are available after RSVP. Plus-one phone numbers are restricted to the submitting brother and event organizers. Public website content such as blog articles and chapter pages is visible without membership.

Service providers and external links

We use Google Firebase services for authentication, databases, file storage, server processing, and notifications, along with Apple services for iOS distribution and push delivery. These providers process information needed to run the service. Opening a linked social-media profile takes you to that platform, whose privacy policy then applies.

Your controls

You can edit supported profile fields, change notification and location settings, block accounts, mute channels, and remove your own supported posts and messages. Downloaded photos, screenshots, or information copied by other users may remain outside our control.

Account deletion and retention

In the app, open Profile → Settings → Privacy & support → Delete account. Confirm your password, type DELETE, and confirm the final prompt. Your account is disabled immediately and a background process removes your authentication account, profile, authored social posts and messages, location, personal settings, and personal event registrations. Other members’ content remains. Shared organization events, documents, and administrative records may remain with the deleted account’s authorship removed. A minimal deletion receipt is retained to prevent an old session from recreating the deleted account.

Historical master-roster and family-tree records are separate fraternity records and may include people who never created a 985 account. Deleting an app account does not remove fraternity membership or automatically erase those historical records. Contact us to request access, correction, or removal of historical information or other information you cannot manage in the app.

Reports are available to administrators while they investigate and resolve concerns. Operational logs and provider backups may persist under the providers’ retention processes. If deletion encounters an interruption, the server retries it; contact support if you need help confirming completion.

Reporting and safety

Use Report post in a post’s options or Report message in the message menu. Administrators receive the report reason, any details you enter, and a copy of the reported content. Your reporter identity is not shown to the reported member through the report feature. For urgent safety concerns, contact appropriate emergency services as well as the organization.

Updates

We may update this policy as the service changes. The effective date above identifies the current version.

Questions or privacy requests?

Email Nu Alpha Phi at national@nualphaphi.com. This is also the support contact for account access, deletion, and content concerns.
